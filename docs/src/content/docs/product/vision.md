# Product Vision

CineFlow Suite exists to reduce the fragmentation in modern film and video production. A typical shoot often depends on disconnected folders, spreadsheets, phone notes, LUT tools, manual backup steps, and improvised handoff documents. CineFlow brings those workflows into one structured, offline desktop application.

## Audience

CineFlow is built for:

- Directors of Photography
- DITs
- Camera operators
- 1st and 2nd ACs
- Producers and creative directors
- Editors and post supervisors
- BTS, art documentation, and multi-camera production teams

## Pillars

### Plan

Pre-production tools reduce on-set friction before the camera rolls: folder structures, reference planning, shot lists, starter camera setup sheets, and visual reports.

### Protect

Production tools keep capture consistent: project-based look setup, camera match analysis, on-set technical checks, normalization presets, and delivery-frame preview.

### Prep

Post-production tools turn raw capture into a verified editorial handoff: safe copy verification, contact sheets, scene blocks, review notes, exports, and organized deliverable packages.
