# Privacy Policy for CineFlow Suite

Effective Date: April 8, 2026

CineFlow Suite is developed with a strict focus on user privacy and data security.

## Local-First Design

CineFlow Suite processes video and photo files locally on your machine for contact sheets, review notes, scene blocks, analysis, and production reports. The app does not upload, store, or transmit your media files to external servers.

Project metadata, clip ratings, and database information are stored locally on your device.

## Permissions

On macOS, CineFlow Suite may request file system access so it can read selected media and save exported reports to locations you choose.

The app may use local loopback networking for internal communication between local components. It does not use this connection to transmit your media to the internet.

## Third-Party Services

CineFlow Suite does not include third-party tracking, advertising SDKs, or analytics SDKs.

## Data Security

Because production media stays on your computer, your system security settings and disk encryption protect your files. Keep your operating system updated and use disk encryption where possible.
